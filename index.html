About the project

This project carried out digital and AI experiments to explore The Electrical Age, making its contents more accessible, discoverable, and ready for historical and computational analysis.

The work was carried out in collaboration with the IET Archives (Institute of Engineering and Technology), with supporting funding provided by the University of Leeds. Technical development was led by Dr Daniel Belteki.
